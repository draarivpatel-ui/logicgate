Section 0. Boolean Expression Grammar & Verification Notes

0.1 Operator Precedence & Syntax

Variables A through P or custom port identifiers. Precedence from highest to lowest: postfix complement A' and prefix ! / ~ / NOT, product & / * / AND / implicit adjacency AB, exclusive-OR ^ / XOR / ~^ / XNOR, and sum | / + / OR. Derived gates ~& (NAND) and ~| (NOR) are supported.

0.2 Karnaugh Map & Petrick's Method

Click any Gray-coded Karnaugh map cell to cycle 0 → 1 → X (don't care). Every modification recomputes prime implicants, essential primes, Petrick's exact branch-and-bound minimum cover, the two-level gate schematic, the logic analyzer waveform, and the Verilog-2001 source.

0.3 IEEE 1364-2001 Verilog Verification
