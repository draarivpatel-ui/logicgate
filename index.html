Quick reference

Expression syntax

Variables A to P or custom names. Operators: & or AND, | or OR, ^ or XOR, ~& or NAND, ~| or NOR, ~^ or XNOR, ! or NOT, parentheses, and implicit AND for adjacent terms. Postfix A' complements a variable. Symbolic forms such as A*B+C and !(A+B) are accepted.

Workbench

Click a Karnaugh map cell to cycle 0 to 1 to X (don't care). Edit the minterm and don't care lists directly. Every change recomputes prime implicants, the minimal SOP and POS, the truth table, the schematic, the waveform, and the Verilog files.

Export

Download or copy the synthesizable Verilog-2001 module and its self-checking testbench. Simulate them with iverilog -o sim.vvp module.v module_tb.v && vvp sim.vvp. Everything runs locally in the browser.
